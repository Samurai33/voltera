## Overview

VoltEra explores a modular infrastructure concept that combines solar and wind
generation, local energy storage and edge-computing capacity. This repository is
the product, architecture and feasibility dossier behind that concept.

It includes the product requirements, proposed technical architecture, bill of
materials, financial model, risk register, compliance notes and operating model.

> **Important:** VoltEra is currently a concept and planning project. Hardware
> specifications, financial projections, regulatory assumptions and marketplace
> mechanics require specialist validation before procurement or deployment.

## System concept

```text
Renewable generation
        ↓
Energy storage and control
        ↓
Edge compute node
        ↓
Secure networking and orchestration
        ↓
Monitoring, capacity planning and resource allocation
```

## Responsible use

Electrical systems, batteries, renewable generation and datacenter infrastructure
involve safety and regulatory risk. Designs in this repository must not replace
work by licensed electrical, structural, environmental, legal or financial
professionals.
